02 / ABOUT

From the signal on a wire
to the software that understands it.

I’m a Computer Engineer at Oral Roberts University focused on embedded systems, automotive electronics, firmware, hardware test, and hardware–software integration.

What pulls me in is the complete system: how power reaches a board, how sensors become bytes, how firmware schedules and moves data, how desktop software diagnoses it, and how the design behaves under real constraints.

My work spans Formula SAE vehicle electronics, C++ and FreeRTOS CAN telemetry, custom PCB design, controls, analog circuits, and Python-based engineering research. I am building toward dependable embedded products that connect software directly to physical systems.
